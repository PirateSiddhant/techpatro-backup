Leadership & Philosophy

A Note from the Founder

How technology, emotional branding, and eastern Nepal's rich tea culture came together to create Biratnagar’s favorite third space.

Author & Visionary

Siddhant Mandal

Founder & Tech Entrepreneur • Chiya Station Biratnagar

"Create a place where emotions meet taste, and tradition blends with modern comfort."

Chiya has always been more than just a beverage for me — it’s an emotion, a pause in a busy day, and an unbreakable bridge between people. When I started Chiya Station in 2019 on Airport Line in Biratnagar, my vision was simple yet deeply meaningful: to create a place where people could slow down, connect, and enjoy truly good tea in a comfortable, welcoming, and hygienic space.

At that time, tea across Biratnagar was largely consumed on hurried roadside street corners. While beloved, it lacked consistency, clean seating, and an atmosphere where students could study, families could gather comfortably, and travelers arriving from Biratnagar Airport could unwind. We set out to change that.

Being deeply involved in technology and multiple tech-driven ventures, I have always believed that innovation should enhance human experiences — not replace them. Chiya Station is a living reflection of that belief. We combined classic earthenware traditions — like our signature kiln-fired Matka Chiya — with modern hospitality, high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, board games (Chess, UNO, Jenga), and a dedicated PlayStation 5 lounge for youth and gamers.

Seeing people travel from far distances across Koshi Province just to enjoy our tea, sit peacefully, or share moments with friends reminds me every single day why this journey began. Every cup poured at Chiya Station carries care, consistency, and character.

This is not just a tea outlet — it is Biratnagar’s living room, built on trust, taste, and togetherness.

Siddhant Mandal
Founder, Chiya Station (Biratnagar - 04, Nepal)
