Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

How we treat your personal contact details, transaction receipts, and communication data.

Key Guarantee: DigiPatro does not sell, rent, or share your contact numbers or chat transcripts with any third parties or advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the essential details needed to fulfill your orders:

  • Your WhatsApp number and display name for delivery and warranty assistance.
  • Your email address (only when a product is directly upgraded on your personal account, e.g. Canva or YouTube).
  • Payment transaction verification screenshots (eSewa / Khalti / Fonepay transaction ID) to confirm receipt.

2. How We Store Your Cart

Items added to your DigiPatro shopping cart are saved strictly in your local device browser storage (localStorage). We do not run persistent cross-site tracking cookies or analytics trackers.

3. Communications

We will only message you regarding the status of your order, delivery credentials, expiration reminders (if opted-in), and warranty assistance. You will not receive spam broadcast lists without consent.
