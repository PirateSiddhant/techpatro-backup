Warranty Commitment

Refund & Replacement Policy

We stand firmly behind every subscription and web project we deliver across Nepal.

100% Replacement Warranty: If an account encounters issues within its validity period, we guarantee a swift fix or replacement.

1. Non-Delivery Refunds

If for any unforeseen technical reason DigiPatro is unable to fulfill your order within 24 hours of payment verification, you are eligible for an immediate, full 100% refund via your original payment method (eSewa / Khalti / Bank transfer).

2. Replacement Warranty Protocol

Due to the nature of digital credentials, accounts that have already been delivered and successfully accessed are not eligible for cash refunds. Instead, they are protected by our full-term Replacement Guarantee:

  • If your assigned profile or account experiences a login error or subscription drop, notify us via WhatsApp with your order details.
  • Our support desk will verify the account status and provide replacement credentials or resolve the issue, usually within 1–4 hours.
  • If an upstream service becomes permanently unavailable and cannot be replaced, a pro-rata credit or refund for the remaining unused days will be issued.

3. Disqualification of Warranty

The replacement warranty becomes void under any of the following circumstances:

  • Attempting to alter the master account login email, password, or subscription tier on shared plans.
  • Sharing profile credentials with third parties beyond the allowed single-profile terms.
  • Violating the explicit terms of use provided at delivery.

4. Website Development Refunds

Deposits paid for website development packages cover upfront design work and server provisioning. If a project is cancelled prior to design approval, a 50% refund of the deposit is provided. Once code development begins, deposits are non-refundable.
